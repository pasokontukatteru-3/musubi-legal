Article 2 (Data Storage with Supabase)

The Service uses Supabase (Supabase Inc., United States) as its backend.

  • User account information, profile information, and AI chat history are stored in Supabase's database.
  • Supabase uses PostgreSQL and implements Row Level Security (RLS), ensuring that each user can only access their own data.
  • Data is transmitted and received through encrypted communications (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Supabase Privacy Policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

Article 3 (Data Transmission to Anthropic Claude API)

The Service uses the Anthropic Claude API (Anthropic, PBC, United States) for its AI features.

  • When using the AI chat feature, user profile information and input messages are sent to Anthropic's servers to generate AI responses.
  • Information sent is used solely for generating AI responses and is not used by Anthropic for model training or learning.
  • Data transmission is conducted through encrypted communications (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Anthropic Privacy Policy: https://www.anthropic.com/privacy

Article 4 (Third-Party Disclosure and Sharing of Data)

The Operator will not provide, sell, or lease users' personal information to third parties except in the following cases:

  1. With the user's consent
  2. As required by law
  3. Provision to service providers necessary for the operation of the Service
